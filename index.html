Keyboard shortcuts

Arrows move the cursor. Ctrl + Space or Ctrl + S saves JSON. Space loads JSON. F2 opens the menu. Tab closes a dialog.

G places ground. 1–8 place a star, spike, enemy, platform, exit, item, scenery or quest. 9 switches maps. 0 deletes. +/− adjusts the selection. I toggles visibility.
